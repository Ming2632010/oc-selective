# TrialSeed agent rules

## Merging

Do **not** merge to `main` until the owner clearly says to merge.

- Build on a `cursor/<feature>-bb40` branch.
- Open or update a **draft** pull request.
- Wait for an explicit confirm such as “merge”, “merge to main”, or “merge when ready”.
- If they only say “build”, “fix”, “looks good”, or “save this”, do not merge.

This applies even after testing, even for small UI fixes, and even if a launch feels urgent.
